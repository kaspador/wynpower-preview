Privacy policy

Our commitment to protecting your personal information.

Our commitment to your privacy

At WynnsLegal, we value the trust you place in us and are dedicated to protecting your personal information. We take every reasonable step to safeguard your details and maintain confidentiality.

This privacy policy explains how we collect, use, store and disclose your personal information in line with the Privacy Act 1988 (Cth) and the Australian Privacy Principles (APPs). We may update this policy from time to time. The current version will always be available on our website.

Information we collect

To provide legal services, we need to collect certain personal information from you. “Personal information” means any details that identify you or could reasonably identify you.

This may include your full name, date of birth, contact details, address, details of your family members and beneficiaries, and information about your assets, liabilities and financial affairs. You are not required to provide this information, but without it we may be unable to act for you.

How we collect your information

We generally collect personal information directly from you, through meetings, phone calls, emails, letters, forms and documents you provide, and enquiries made through our website. In some cases, and with your consent, we may obtain information from third parties such as family members, financial institutions or other professionals.

How we use your information

We use your personal information to verify your identity, communicate with you, provide the legal services you have asked for, and meet our legal and professional obligations.

We only disclose your information to third parties when necessary to carry out your instructions or as required by law. This may include courts and government bodies, financial institutions, other professionals involved in your matter, and IT and cloud software providers that support our practice.

We keep records for as long as required by law and our professional obligations. Original wills and other important documents may be held in safe custody for longer at your request.

Protecting your information

We use a combination of physical, electronic and administrative safeguards to prevent unauthorised access, loss or misuse of your information. These include confidentiality obligations, restricted office access, password-protected systems, firewalls, antivirus software, secure servers and secure destruction of paper records.

If you choose to communicate with us by email, please note that internet transmissions are not always secure and are at your own risk.

Data breach notification

Under the Notifiable Data Breaches scheme, we must notify affected individuals and the Office of the Australian Information Commissioner (OAIC) if a data breach is likely to result in serious harm. We maintain a data breach response plan to meet this obligation.

Accessing or correcting your information

You can ask to access or correct your personal information at any time by calling (03) 9742 3192 or emailing info@wynpower.com.au. We will respond within a reasonable time. An administrative fee may apply for extensive data retrieval.
