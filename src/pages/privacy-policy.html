Privacy policy

Our commitment to protecting your personal information.

Our commitment to your privacy

At Werribee Accounting Service, we value the trust you place in us and are dedicated to protecting your personal information. We understand the importance of privacy and take every reasonable step to safeguard your details and maintain confidentiality.

This privacy policy explains how we collect, use, store and disclose your personal information in line with the Privacy Act 1988 (Cth) and the Australian Privacy Principles (APPs). We may update this policy from time to time to reflect changes in law or in our business practices. The current version will always be available on our website.

Information we collect

To deliver quality accounting, taxation and financial advisory services, we need to collect certain personal information from you. “Personal information” means any details that identify you or could reasonably identify you.

This may include your full name, date of birth, contact details, residential or business address, bank and superannuation information, and taxation or financial details (past and present). You are not required to provide this information, but without it we may be unable to deliver our services.

How we collect your information

We generally collect personal information directly from you. In some cases, and with your consent, we may obtain information from third parties.

We may collect information through face-to-face meetings, phone calls, documents or forms you provide (digital or paper), emails, letters, uploads to our systems, authorised third-party services, website forms, event registrations or social media interactions. We may also receive information incidentally, for example when your bank details appear on a payment made to our accounts. All information collected is managed according to this policy.

How we use your information

We use your personal information for legitimate business purposes, including verifying your identity, communicating with you, providing agreed services, advising you of service updates or related offerings, providing support, and meeting legal or regulatory requirements. By engaging our firm, you consent to the collection and use of your information as described in this policy or as otherwise authorised by you.

We only disclose your information to third parties when necessary to deliver our services or as required by law. Third parties may include bookkeepers, auditors, financial service providers, IT contractors, and cloud software partners such as Seamless SMSF and independent auditor Michael McHenry.

We may store information on secure cloud-based systems. Records are kept for at least seven years after our services are completed, or longer if required for ongoing work or compliance purposes.

Protecting your information

We take the protection of your information seriously and use a combination of physical, electronic and administrative safeguards to prevent unauthorised access, loss or misuse. These include confidentiality agreements for all staff, regular privacy training, restricted office access, password-protected systems, firewalls, antivirus software, secure servers and shredding of paper records.

If you choose to communicate with us by email, please note that internet transmissions are not always secure and are at your own risk.

Data breach notification

Under the Notifiable Data Breaches (NDB) scheme, introduced by the Privacy Amendment (Notifiable Data Breaches) Act 2017, we must notify affected individuals and the Office of the Australian Information Commissioner (OAIC) if a data breach is likely to result in serious harm.

To comply with this, Werribee Accounting Service has:

  • appointed a Privacy Officer
  • developed a Data Breach Response Plan
  • reviewed and audited our data management systems
  • added privacy notices to client forms
  • implemented opt-out options in marketing
  • trained staff
  • established an ongoing compliance program

Marketing communications
