Tax agent disclosure

Client information disclosure for Werribee Accounting Service.

As a registered tax agent, Theresa Ann Sgambaro trading as Werribee Accounting Service operates under the Tax Agent Services Act 2009, which includes the Code of Professional Conduct set by the Tax Practitioners Board (TPB).

We are required to provide the following information so you are fully informed about our registration, our responsibilities and your rights as a client.

1. Matters you should be aware of

We must tell you about any matters that may influence your decision to engage, or continue to engage, us for tax agent services.

There are currently no matters to disclose.

2. The TPB register

You can verify our registration, or check the details of any registered tax or BAS agent, on the TPB’s public register at tpb.gov.au/public-register. The register also explains how to search by name or registration number.

3. If you have a complaint

If you are unhappy with any part of our service, please contact us directly so we can resolve the issue promptly and professionally.

If we cannot resolve the issue to your satisfaction, you can lodge a complaint with the TPB through its secure online form at myprofile.tpb.gov.au/complaints. More information is available at tpb.gov.au/complaints.

4. Code of Professional Conduct

As a registered practitioner, we follow the TPB Code of Professional Conduct. Our obligations include:

  • Acting in your best interests
  • Providing services competently and on time
  • Managing conflicts of interest
  • Acting lawfully, honestly and with integrity
  • Taking reasonable care in your tax matters

As a client, your responsibilities include:

  • Being honest and accurate with the information you provide
  • Supplying records on time
  • Advising us of any relevant changes
  • Cooperating with your tax agent as needed

To learn more, see tpb.gov.au/code-professional-conduct and tpb.gov.au/obligations.

5. Reportable events

We confirm there are no reportable events.

6. Conditions on our registration

We must tell you if the TPB has placed any restrictions or conditions on our registration.

There are no conditions on our registration.

This information is current as of 29 September 2025.
