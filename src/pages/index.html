What we do

Our services

All services

  • Individual tax returns

    Salary and wage returns, plus rental property, capital gains and other schedules. Completed at your appointment wherever possible.

  • Business accounting & tax

    Tax returns for sole traders, partnerships, companies and trusts, along with management accounting and fixed annual fees for ongoing clients.

  • BAS & activity statements

    Business and instalment activity statements, GST and PAYG compliance, and PAYG variations.

  • Business set-up

    Company and trust registration, ABN and business name applications, WorkCover registration and corporate register maintenance.

  • Self managed super funds

    Annual accounts and tax returns for SMSFs, working with an independent auditor.

  • Wills & estate planning

    Wills, powers of attorney, testamentary trusts and special disability trusts, prepared by our solicitor.
