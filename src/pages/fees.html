Good to know

  • Returns are lodged with the ATO once fees are paid in full. A $55 account fee applies if fees are not prepaid.
  • Set fees cover preparation only. Data processing, ledger reconciliation and tax calculations are charged on a fee-for-time basis.
  • Management accounting, business tax returns, ATO audit work and client-initiated amendments are charged on a fee-for-time basis.
  • We can negotiate a fixed annual fee with existing business clients.
  • Estate planning fees include GST and the initial consultation.
