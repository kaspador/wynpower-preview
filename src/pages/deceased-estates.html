Help for executors

Being named as an executor is a responsibility, and many people are unsure where to start. We explain your duties, help you gather the information you need, and take care of the legal work so the estate is finalised correctly.

  • Advice to executors on their duties
  • Applying for a grant of probate
  • Letters of administration where there is no valid will
  • Collecting assets and paying debts
  • Distributing the estate to beneficiaries

Survivorship applications

Where a property was owned jointly, it usually passes to the surviving owner. A survivorship application records the change on the title so the property is in the survivor’s name. We prepare and lodge the application for you.

Property searches

We can carry out title searches to confirm what property the deceased owned and how it was held.
