About our business

Personalised financial consulting, tax accounting and legal services in Werribee and Melbourne’s western suburbs since 1992.

Over thirty years in Werribee

Werribee Accounting Service has provided personalised tax and accounting services throughout Werribee since 1992. With that experience behind us, no issue is too large or too complex for us to handle, whether you need help with your own affairs or at a corporate level.

We act as tax planners and accounting advisers to individuals, families and businesses. Come and see us, and find out how easy accounting can be when we take care of the detail for you.

Accounting and legal, side by side

Werribee Legal Service provides legal services to clients in Melbourne and the surrounding suburbs. Because our principal is both an accountant and a solicitor, estate planning and other legal work is done with a full understanding of your financial position.

We are a small team of dedicated professionals. Our licensed accountants and lawyers work to the highest professional standards, giving clients the specialised accounting and legal support they need so they can focus on their business and personal lives.

Professional standards

  • Registered tax agent under the Tax Agent Services Act 2009, bound by the Tax Practitioners Board Code of Professional Conduct.
  • Liability limited by a scheme approved under Professional Standards Legislation.
  • Personal information handled under the Privacy Act 1988 (Cth) and the Australian Privacy Principles.
