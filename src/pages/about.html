About WynnsLegal

Providing legal services to the Wyndham community since 2008.

A focused, local practice

WynnsLegal is a Wyndham law practice focused on wills, estate planning, powers of attorney and deceased estates. Since 2008 we have helped local individuals and families put their affairs in order, and supported executors when a loved one has died.

Because we concentrate on one area of law, we can give clear, practical advice and handle your matter efficiently. Most of our work is done for a published fixed fee, so you know the cost before you begin.
