Werribee Accounting Service

Accounting & tax

Individuals

Most salary and wage returns can be completed at your appointment. Where you have extra income or investments, we prepare the schedules you need and make sure everything is lodged correctly with the ATO.

  • Individual income tax returns
  • Rental property, capital gains tax and business income schedules
  • Amendments to prior-year returns
  • PAYG variations
  • Assistance with ATO reviews, audits and objections

Businesses

From sole traders to companies and trusts, we take care of your tax and compliance obligations and give you advice you can act on. Existing business clients can arrange a fixed annual fee.

  • Company, trust and partnership tax returns
  • Business and instalment activity statements (BAS and IAS)
  • GST and PAYG compliance
  • Management accounting
  • Tax planning

Setting up a business or structure

  • Company registration
  • Discretionary (family) and unit trusts
  • ABN and business name registration
  • WorkCover registration
  • Corporate register maintenance and amendments

Self managed super funds

We prepare annual accounts and tax returns for self managed super funds. Every SMSF must be independently audited each year. We work with an independent auditor, whose fee is paid to them directly.

Werribee Legal Service

Wills & estate planning

A will and the right powers of attorney make sure your wishes are followed and the people you trust can act for you if you can’t. Your documents are prepared by our solicitor, who can also take your tax and financial position into account.

  • Standard wills for individuals and couples
  • Testamentary trusts
  • Special disability trusts
  • Enduring and general (non-enduring) powers of attorney
  • Medical treatment decision maker appointments
  • Advance care directives and enduring power of guardianship
  • Survivorship applications

For other matters, such as binding financial agreements or lease transfers, we provide a written costs estimate after an initial consultation.
